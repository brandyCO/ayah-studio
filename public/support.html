Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Signing in with Apple or Google only keeps your progress the same on all your devices and lets you join weekly leagues.

My progress doesn't show on my other phone

Sign in with the same account on both, then open Me and pull down to sync. Progress is never deleted; it is joined from all your devices.

How do I listen without internet?

Tap the star beside a reciter and choose what to download. Surahs you have listened to before are also kept on the phone.

How do I make a reel?

Open a surah, long-press an ayah (drag to select more), then tap "Turn into reel". Export saves a 9:16 video with the recitation synced to the words.

How do I delete my account?

Me → ⚙ → Account → Delete account. Everything linked to you is removed. More details
