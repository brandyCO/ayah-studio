Privacy

Ayah Studio · last updated 8 October 2026

Without an account

Everything stays on your device: your bookmarks, reflections, the pages you read (for your sky), settings, reel drafts, the photos and videos you add, and the recitations kept for offline use. Nothing is sent to us. Exported videos are saved on your device and only shared where you choose to share them.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It keeps the following the same on your devices, stored with Supabase (our database provider):

  • your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account;
  • your bookmarks (which ayat and when);
  • your reflections — the private notes you write on an ayah, with their dates. Only you can read them; they are never shared with anyone, including people in a circle with you;
  • the pages you marked as memorised (revision lamps): when each was last revised and its step;
  • your app settings (for example the reciter and the look of your last reel, and the page you were reading);
  • your reel drafts (which ayat, reciter, text and scene settings) — not their preview pictures, and never your own photos or videos, which stay on the device where you added them.
  • everything else the app keeps about your use of it, so another device carries on where you left off: your Learn path and lessons, page memory and weak words, the adhkar and du'a you said, your room, character and styles, Ramadan records, search history, recently read pages and your settings. Your locations (prayer times, the Ramadan reminder), downloaded recitations and pages, your own photos, videos and voice recordings, and everything in the Kids space stay on the device.

Only you can read or change this data. We do not sell it, show ads, or share it with anyone else.

Khatm circles

If you start or join a Khatm circle, its members see the name you give yourself in that circle, which juz you took and when you finished it. Nothing else of yours is shared — not your reading, bookmarks or reflections. When you leave a circle, your unfinished juz becomes free again; when you delete your account, you leave every circle first (a circle you started passes to its next member).

Revision lamps

The pages you mark as memorised, with the day you last revised each one and its revision step, are kept on your device and, when you are signed in, synced to your account like your bookmarks. Only you can see them.

Ramadan mode

Your Ramadan plan, the pages you read and the ayat you came back to are kept on your device and, when you are signed in, synced to your account. If you use your location for the reminder before Maghrib, it is rounded to about a kilometre and also stays on your device; it is only used to work out the time of Maghrib.

Me: streak, goals, badges, prayer times

Your daily streak, reading stats, daily goals (including the names of goals you write yourself), dhikr counts, badges, the Light you gathered and the pages you read are kept on your device. When you are signed in, they are also saved to your account so your other devices show the same streak and goals; only you can read them, and they are in Export my data. Pages are counted on the device (a page counts after it has been open for the reading time you choose). Prayer times are worked out on your device from your location, rounded to about a kilometre; the location is never sent anywhere. The home-screen widgets read the same data on your phone.

If your streak goes out and you ask a friend to relight it (signed in with Google), we store your first name, the length of the streak and the days missed, for 48 hours of relighting; when a friend relights it, their first name is stored with it so you can see who it was. Nothing about your reading is sent. Deleting your account deletes these too.

Ranks (signed in with Google): each day's totals of pages counted, minutes of recitation, surahs finished and reels made, with the name and drawn avatar you choose, are sent so leagues and boards can be shown; "Keep my reading between me and Allah" hides your name and keeps you off the global board. Dhikr, adhkar, du'a and Light are never sent for ranking. A friend challenge stores who is in it and when it started. A bedtime pact stores who is in it, when it started and which of its seven nights each member marked as kept; members see each other's name, avatar and crescents. A lamp on the lamp wall stores the first name you write, him or her, the du'a you picked, its size and when it was lit; every signed-in player can see it, and your own name only if you chose to show it. Who said Ameen under a lamp is stored but never shown, only the count. You can take your lamp down at any time. Deleting your account deletes all of these.

Gifts

When you send a gift (a few ayat as a link), we store the reference (for example 2:255), the reciter, the look you chose (colours, effect, a built-in background), the name you sign it with and your short message. Anyone who has the link can open it, without an account; nobody can browse or search gifts. You see how many times your gift was opened and the replies sent to it. You can delete a gift at any time (☰ → Gifts), and deleting your account deletes all your gifts. Gifts you open are remembered on your device only. A reply sent without a Google account uses a temporary anonymous session.

Ayah walls (no longer offered)

The ayah wall has been removed from the app. Walls and entries made earlier are still kept as described before — only their host and the person who added an entry can see it — and they are included in Export my data. Deleting your account deletes your walls and entries.

Kids space and family circles

The Kids space keeps everything on your device: the child's name (if you give one), the surahs they have learned, and settings. It has no account, no ads and nothing in it is sent anywhere. Children never have accounts.

If a signed-in parent creates a family circle, its members (people the parent invites) see the first name or nickname the parent gives each child, which short surahs the child is learning or has learned, and the short du'a notes members leave for a child (with the author's name in the circle). When the parent links the Kids space on their device to a child, lit lanterns are shared with that circle and notes are shown to the child there. A child's parent (or the circle's owner) can rename or remove the child, which deletes their surahs and notes; deleting the circle deletes everything in it.

Your choices

  • Export: ☰ → Account → Export my data gives you everything stored with your account as a file.
  • Delete: ☰ → Account → Delete account removes your account and all data synced to it at once. The copy on your device stays until you remove the app or clear its data.
  • Sign out at any time; the app keeps working on your device.

Other services the app uses

  • Recitations, word timings and text data come from Quran.com (QDC) and everyayah.com; your device downloads them directly.
  • When you search the free background library, your search goes to Pixabay (or Pexels) through our small proxy, which keeps no record of who searched.
  • Tasmi' recordings ("Record me" in Learn) stay on your device and are never uploaded. Follow my voice uses your phone's speech recognition to turn your recitation into text while you recite; the app keeps no audio and no text. In the Android app this is the phone's own recogniser (on the device when Android has its Arabic language pack); in a web browser it is the browser's, and Chrome sends the sound to Google to recognise it. When you are signed in and our harakat check is on, the phone's recogniser is not used: each part you recite (a few seconds, cut at a pause) is sent to our own server on Google Cloud with the words of the passage. The server follows the words and checks the harakat in memory, answers, and forgets it: no audio or text is stored or logged. It is limited to one hour of recitation a day per account.
  • Help improve the harakat check (off unless you turn it on; never in the Kids space): when it is on, a short clip (up to 3 seconds: a word the check flagged, or now and then one it did not, with the words next to it) and your answer at the check are kept with your account so that the teachers who review them can say whether the harakat were right, and the check can be tuned. Teachers never see who shared a clip. At most 40 clips a day. Me → ⚙ → Help improve the harakat check turns it off or deletes every clip you shared; deleting your account deletes them too. Their words and labels are in Export my data.
